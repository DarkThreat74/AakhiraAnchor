Waqt is a prayer-centered life tracker that anchors your day around the five daily prayers. Schedule events, track goals, and stay accountable to your prayer practice — all in one place.

PRAYER ACCOUNTABILITY
• Automatic prayer time calculation based on your location
• Check in when you pray — the app tracks your streak and consistency
• Gentle reminders at the start, middle, and end of each prayer window
• No penalties for missed check-ins — unmarked prayers are assumed prayed
• Qadaa tracker for missed prayers, with a simple estimator to get started

CALENDAR
• Day and month views with a scrollable hour grid
• Prayer windows render as background bands — never blocking your events
• Overlapping events stack side-by-side (no warnings, no blocking)
• Natural language event entry (type "Meeting tomorrow at 3pm" and it parses)

GOALS
• Create goals as a simple list or as a branching tree map
• Break big goals into smaller sub-goals with unlimited nesting
• Add details and descriptions to any goal
• Check off completed goals with a single tap
• Export your goal map as an image or PDF
• Share a read-only public link to your goals

DAILY PRACTICE
• Daily Huddle: a short task list to start your day with intention
• Daily lessons from a curated, human-reviewed content bank
• Dhikr counter with preset sequences and target counts
• Curated talks library with external links to authenticated sources

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) on app launch
• Trusted-device login — no password needed on your own devices
• Account deletion with full PII anonymization
• No tracking, no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics
• Works offline — your data is cached and synced when you reconnect

Waqt is free. Prayer accountability is and always will be free. The app does not process payments for religious obligations.
